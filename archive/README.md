# Archive

`caustic-lamp-v1/` is the earlier caustic lamp, designed in SolidWorks in June 2026: base, stem, top and retainer parts
(`.SLDPRT` and `.STL`) with two assemblies, `Caustic Lamp 1.0` and `Caustic Lamp 2.0`. It's kept here for reference.
The current design is the bench rig in [`cad/`](../cad/).
